This update optimises network requests. Categories and other image metadata should now load much faster, without long delays.

Further improvements:

- Unneeded image files are now properly cleared up after uploads and when drafts are deleted.

- The licence selector now shows the version of CC licences (e.g. “CC BY 4.0” instead of just “CC BY”).

- Suggested categories and places close to the camera location are now ranked more sensibly. Previously, highly relevant categories sometimes appeared further down the list. (See the git commit for details ;)
